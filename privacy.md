# Privacy Policy

**FleetOptima LLC** · Last updated: November 6, 2026

This is how FleetOptima handles your information, in plain words. If something here doesn't answer your question, email **support@fleetoptima.org** and a real person answers.

## The short version
- Your company's data is **your company's**. We use it to run FleetOptima for you, and that's it.
- **We don't sell your data, show you ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.**
- A few outside services do part of the work (listed below), and each one only gets what it needs.
- You can ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be deleted, at any time.
- **If you're a tenant:** your landlord's account holds what you send, and your landlord can see it. See **If you're a tenant** below. <!-- for: landlord -->

## What we collect

**Your account.** Your name, username, email address, your role, the groups you're in, which assets you're assigned to, your email alert choices, and when you last logged in. Your password is stored only as a one-way scrambled form (a hash), so nobody at FleetOptima can read it. <!-- for: fleet -->
**Your account.** Your name, username, email address, your role, the groups you're in, which properties you're assigned to, your email alert choices, and when you last logged in. Your password is stored only as a one-way scrambled form (a hash), so nobody at FleetOptima can read it. <!-- for: landlord -->

**Your company's records.** Everything your company puts into FleetOptima: assets (names, VINs, serial numbers, make and model, readings like miles or hours), who is assigned to which asset, work orders and their notes, how urgent they are, outside services (who did the work and what it cost), parts, stock, costs, maintenance rules, and who did what and when. <!-- for: fleet -->
**Your company's records.** Everything your company puts into FleetOptima: properties (names and addresses), who is assigned to which property, maintenance requests (what's wrong, how urgent, who sent it, and notes), outside services (who did the work and what it cost), costs, maintenance rules, and who did what and when. <!-- for: landlord -->

**Feedback you send us.** When you use the **Yell at us** button, we keep your message along with the page you were on, the app version, and your device type (for example "web · android"), so we can find the problem.

**A record of requests to our servers.** For security, every request to FleetOptima's servers is logged with the time, the account it came from (or none), the internet address it came from (IP address), the kind of device or browser (its "user agent"), the page or action, and whether it worked. We use this to spot break-in attempts and fix problems. **We never log what you typed, your password, or the contents of your records.** These logs are deleted after **90 days**.

**Photos you take in the app.** When you use the camera to read a VIN, a mileage or a part number off a photo, the photo is sent to our server and on to the reading service (see below), the text comes back, and **the photo is thrown away. We don't keep it.** <!-- for: fleet -->

## Emails we send
- **Account emails**: invites, password links and email checks. These go out when you or your company's Admins ask for them.
- **Email alerts**, only when they're turned on for you (**App settings → Email alerts**, where you can turn each one off): a new work order, or a change to one you reported (started, finished, cancelled). An alert includes the work order's title, what's wrong, the asset's name, who reported it, and a link to it. <!-- for: fleet -->
- **Email alerts**, only when they're turned on for you (**App settings → Email alerts**, where you can turn each one off): a new maintenance request, or a change to one you sent (started, finished, cancelled). An alert includes the request's title, what's wrong, the property's name, who sent it, and a link to it. For landlords, alerts about new requests start on; tenants only get alerts they turn on themselves. <!-- for: landlord -->
- Every alert has a link at the bottom that turns that kind of alert off, without logging in. We never email you about something you did yourself.

## What's stored on your device
- **Staying logged in:** one cookie that keeps you logged in on that device. Page code can't read it, it's only sent to our login service, and it ends when you log out, use **Sign out everywhere**, change your password, or don't use FleetOptima on that device for 30 days.
- **App settings:** your light/dark choice, which help notes you've closed, how many times you've opened Home today (so the greeting shrinks after the first), and similar small settings, kept in your browser.

No advertising cookies, no tracking cookies, no third-party analytics.

## Outside services we use
| Service | What it does for us | What it gets |
|---|---|---|
| **Microsoft Azure** (US data centers) | Runs FleetOptima's servers and database | All of the above, stored for us |
| **Microsoft Azure AI Vision** | Reads text off photos you take in the app | The photo, for that one read |
| **Microsoft Azure Communication Services** | Sends our emails (invites, password links, email checks, email alerts) | Your name, email address, and the email's contents |
| **NHTSA** (U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration) | Looks up vehicle details from a VIN | The VIN, sent from your browser | <!-- for: fleet -->

| **GitHub** | Hosts the user guide the app shows you | Your IP address and browser info, when you open Help |

We don't give or sell your information to anyone else, except if the law makes us (for example, a valid court order), and we'll tell you if we're allowed to.

## Who at your company can see what
Your company's Admins decide who sees and changes what, through roles and groups. Your Admins can see the people in your company and the records they create. FleetOptima staff can access your company's data to set it up for you, fix problems, keep the service secure, and when you ask us to.

**People assigned to the same asset see the work orders on it,** including who reported each one. *For example:* two operators who share a truck both see the problems reported on it. <!-- for: fleet -->

## If you're a tenant
- **Your landlord's account holds what you send.** Your landlord, and the people they give access to (like their maintenance staff), can see your name, your email address, the property you're assigned to, and every request you send, including what you wrote and when.
- **People on the same property see its requests.** If you share a home, your roommates see the requests sent on it, including yours and your name on them. That way two people don't report the same leak. They don't see your email address or anything about you beyond that.
- **Nobody outside your landlord's account sees any of it**, except FleetOptima staff for the reasons above.
- **Your landlord decides how long their records are kept** while they use FleetOptima. If you want a copy of, or a change to, something you sent, ask your landlord first; you can also email us, and we'll work it out with them.

## How long we keep things
- Your company's records stay as long as your company uses FleetOptima.
- **Deleting your account** stops you from logging in and removes you from your company's lists. The work you did (work orders, notes, history) stays with your company's records, because those belong to the company. <!-- for: fleet -->
- **Deleting your account** stops you from logging in and removes you from your company's lists. The requests you sent, and their notes and history, stay with your landlord's records, because those belong to the account they were sent to. <!-- for: landlord -->
- If your company stops using FleetOptima, we keep its data for **90 days** in case you come back or need a copy, then delete it, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.
- Request logs: 90 days. Backups roll over within a few weeks.

## Your choices
- **A copy of your data:** email us and we'll send your company's records in a common file format.
- **Fix something:** most things you can change in the app. For anything else, email us.
- **Delete:** delete your own account from **Account settings**, or email us to delete your company's data.
- **Email alerts:** turn them on or off any time in **App settings → Email alerts**, or with the link at the bottom of any alert.
- We answer within **30 days**, usually much sooner.

## Security
Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed. Login links work once and expire. Too many wrong passwords lock an account for a while. Admins can sign anyone in their company out of every device at once. No system is perfect: if we ever learn your data was exposed, we'll tell you quickly and say what happened.

## Changes
When we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app or by email, and the app asks you to agree again the next time you log in. The date at the top shows the latest version. **If we ever want to use information you already gave us in a new way, we'll ask you first.**
